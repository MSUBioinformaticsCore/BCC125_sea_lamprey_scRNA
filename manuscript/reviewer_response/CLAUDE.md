# Working preferences

## Writing style

- **Never use em dashes (—).** Not in prose, not in drafts, not in headings, not in tables,
  not in code comments. Use commas, semicolons, colons, parentheses, or separate sentences.
- Do not substitute en dashes (–) or spaced hyphens ( - ) as a workaround. Restructure the
  sentence instead.
- Be concise and direct. Cut words that do not change the meaning.
- Push back when an idea is wrong rather than accommodating it.
- **Use US English spelling.** characterized, recognized, labeling, judgment, program,
  analyze, behavior, toward. Not the British forms.

## Code

- Primary language is R.

## Domain notes

- Non-model organism RNA-seq, sea lamprey (*Petromyzon marinus*), assembly kPetMar1.pri
  (GCF_010993605.1), NCBI Annotation Release 100.
- Do not assert annotation provenance, marker-gene origin, or orthology relationships
  without verifying against the actual files. Flag unverified claims explicitly rather than
  filling gaps with plausible assumptions.
